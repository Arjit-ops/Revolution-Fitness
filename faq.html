FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Revolution Fitness in Jodhpur.

Does Revolution Fitness offer a free trial?

Yes, Revolution Fitness offers a free trial pass so new members can try the gym before joining a membership plan.

What are Revolution Fitness's gym timings?

Revolution Fitness is open from 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays, and 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM on weekends.

Does Revolution Fitness have personal trainers?

Yes, Revolution Fitness offers one-on-one personal training led by founder Sandeep Tak and the coaching team, tailored to strength, weight loss, and conditioning goals.

Is parking available at Revolution Fitness?

Yes, Revolution Fitness offers dedicated on-site parking for all members at its Ratanada Circle location in Jodhpur.

Where is Revolution Fitness located in Jodhpur?

Revolution Fitness is located at 7-A Defense Lab Road, Ratanada Circle, Ratanada, Jodhpur, Rajasthan 342011.
